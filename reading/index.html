MindNav

Typing with the brain alone.

An end-to-end EEG brain–computer interface that reads mental intent in real time and drives a hands-free scanning keyboard. Three electrodes feed a BioAmp amplifier and an Arduino; the pipeline extracts 42 features and classifies them with classical and deep-learning models in PyTorch.

I worked on the neuroscience behind the signal, contributed to the acquisition-to-inference pipeline, and co-developed the decoding. The team also co-authored a review of BCI and EEG technologies.
